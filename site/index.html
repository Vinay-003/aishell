THE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The assistant proposes.
You decide.

Native mode is an opt-in Bash session, not a pretend terminal. Your aliases, functions, virtualenvs, job control, and sourced configuration still belong to your shell.

Cliffy puts a draft in your editable command buffer with Ctrl+G. Review or change it before Enter. Cliffy does not execute that draft for you.

TWO WAYS INTO THE WORK

Your workflow, not ours.

01

Real Bash, with a second opinion.

Launch cliffy --shell bash when you want a native Bash session. This mode is opt-in, keeps its own state, and shares your files with the rest of your system. It is not a sandbox and does not edit your global shell profile.

Native-mode notes →
02

The original Cliffy assistant.

Run cliffy for the Python assistant experience: ghost-text suggestions, % commands, %% guided work, and %%% coding mode. Its state and limits differ from native Bash; choose the mode that fits the job.
